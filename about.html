About Wedding.Plan

We help couples in Myanmar plan a beautiful wedding day — from vision and budget to packages and every service detail.

💗

Our story

Wedding Plan began as a second-year Computer Science group project with one goal: make wedding planning simple, personal, and stress-free. We believe every couple deserves a clear path from dream to celebration.

✨

What we offer

Choose your wedding vision, set your date and budget, pick a Basic, Standard, or Premium package, then select one item from each of our eight services — dress, food, shoes, accessories, cake, bouquets, decorations, and venue.

🤍

How it works

Browse packages without logging in. Build your plan step by step. Log in only when you are ready to confirm your booking. After that, view and edit your saved plan anytime from My plan.

📍

Who we serve

We design for couples across Myanmar who want elegant options at transparent bundle prices — whether you are planning an intimate gathering or a grand celebration.

Meet the team

Wedding Plan is built by Group 3 (2025) — students passionate about clean design, useful calculations, and a smooth user experience for real wedding planning.

  • Budget-aware package recommendations
  • Eight wedding services in one bundle
  • Save, review, and update your booking
